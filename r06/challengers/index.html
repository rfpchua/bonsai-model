Why the 18.7% headline was withdrawn

The first C03 result had two living-soffit lights physically overlapping. Their anchors were only 6 cm apart. Recalculating the saved light arrays reproduced the number, but the arrangement cannot be accepted as buildable. C02 also placed four overhead anchors outside the actual mounting slabs. Both results were rejected. The corrected v2 results now appear in the comparison above; the original gains remain excluded.

What C01 changes

C01 re-aims four overheads at the existing anchors. It retains five overheads (3 ×120 W +2 ×80 W), six standard Targetti heads, no Maxis, and 719.2 W. Pole positions and aims remain fixed. Canopy coverage and the weakest-pad measure improve, while peak electric PPFD falls. Courtyard eye-level spill rises from 3,241 to 3,640 lux, within the inherited 3,666 lux comparison ceiling; that ceiling is not a comfort certification.

Typical-day mean is 4.671 for R06 and 4.965 for C01. Those values include more modelled daylight and must not be compared with dull-day means. No new hardware is needed for C01; re-aiming and commissioning labor is unquoted.

How to choose between the repaired results

C03 v2 has the highest tested mean: 4.616 (+15.39%), at the same719.2 W. It moves/re-aims overheads and re-aims pole heads. Whole-canopy≥4 coverage rises to52.2%, and the weakest-pad and foyer p10 measures improve versus R06. However, peak electric PPFD rises from260.5 to456.1 (+75.1%); p99 rises from212.8 to288.2 (+35.5%). C01 has lower peaks, a better weakest-pad p10, and more foyer-face coverage above4. C03 is the mean-maximizing candidate, not a demonstrated longevity winner.

C02 v2 reaches4.123 (+3.06%) at679.2 W, using2×120 W +3×80 W and six Mediums. Its weakest-pad p10 falls to1.465 and foyer≥4 coverage falls to47.6%. It misses the7% target and trades some coverage for lower electrical load; purchase and relocation costs are unquoted.

Could one stronger light replace the overlapping pair?

Philips lists a150 W,30°/4000 K DMX UniFlood M G2:13,725 lm,569×140×149 mm,6.8 kg. Its width/height match the120 W format, with83 mm extra length, making it the first stronger fit candidate. A200 W DMX version gives18,525 lm,403×304×231 mm,11.5 kg. The rejected120+80 W pair totalled17,920 lm, so the200 W is closer in total luminous output; beam pattern and delivered DLI still need testing. Neither stronger replacement has been simulated or confirmed mountable. A wider body can overhang only if the bracket, fasteners, support and tilt clearances permit it.

What remains provisional

The additional review checks saved source totals, actual underside anchor footprints and all fixture-body pairs. It does not certify screw patterns, brackets, hinges, supports, waterproofing or installation. Philips 120 W output uses scaled 80 W photometry; shared daylight assumptions, lack of fixture self-shadow and field PAR/thermal verification remain relevant. A 7% DLI gain does not mean 7% more lifespan.

These are best results within the tested candidate sets, not proof of a global optimum. Rejected versions are archived locally. R06 and the shared comparison physics remain unchanged.
