What cutting a Targetti changes

Relative to the best six-head result, the best five-head result changes mean by -0.264DLI (-4.90%) and B6 mean by -0.135. These are independently optimized layouts, not a fixed-layout ablation. B6 — the lowest pendant—is the key pad to watch. Its best five-head ≥4coverage is 0.0%, versus 0.1% for best six.

Removing one Targetti avoids approximately $1,000 under the owner's working estimate, before 150 W upgrade, bracket and installation costs. That is an approximate fixture saving, not a verified net installed saving. No supplier quote for the 150 W premium was supplied. How much comes from wattage alone?

On the exact best-six C04 layout, reverting all three150W fixtures to120W reduces mean from5.375 to5.041. Thus the power-only uplift is 6.61%; the larger gain against C03 also includes repositioning and head redistribution. On the balanced five-head layout, power-only uplift is 7.28% (4.604→4.939). This is an exact linear ablation within the declared scaled-IES model, not measured150W photometry.

Reproducibility and scope

Informed finite search, seed 6114; reuses disclosed C03 responses, then adds local overhead/pole variations and small 80 W alternative hosts. Neither a blind test nor proof of global optimum. The no-upgrade optimized control separates extra search benefit from hardware changes. A further housing-versus-architecture check rejects glazing, frame and slab intersections in all C04 rows. The additional check found C03’s living housing intersects foyer/living glazing; C03’s4.616 is a historical numerical reference, not an installable arrangement as drawn. R06 passed this added housing check. Earlier provisional C04 candidates failing this check were rejected and archived before publication. All selected final responses are freshly recomputed; per-fixture cache agreement and all-pair body, service envelope and base proxy checks are saved.
